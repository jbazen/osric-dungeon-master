# OSRIC — Wilderness Encounter Tables

*OSRIC, Chapter IV (Dungeons, Towns and Wildernesses), book pages 179–191.*
*13 terrain encounter tables — Aquatic, Arctic, Desert, Forest, Graveyard, Hills, Jungle, Lost World, Marine, Mountains, Plains, Rural, Tundra — each a 1d20 roll across two 6-column sub-tables (normally Airborne/Animal/Dragon/Giant/Human/Humanoid and Monster/NPC/Undead/Invertebrates/Water/Special; Lost World substitutes Large Carnivore/Large Herbivore for Dragon/Giant and Other Dinos for Monster; Tundra's second table reads Vermin for Invertebrates) — plus the Dinosaur Encounter Table and its 8 linked sub-tables (Tyrannosaurids & Allosaurids, Hadrosaurids, Sauropods, Ceratopsians, Other Dinosaurs I, Other Dinosaurs II, Marine Dinosaurs, Flying Dinosaurs). 22 headed sections, 575 table rows.*
