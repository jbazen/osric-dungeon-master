# Items & Loot

Reference: `osric-srd/markdown/04 alignment money equipment.md` for mundane equipment/pricing, and
the monster entries (`osric-srd/markdown/17a`–`23b`) for each monster's Treasure Type.

## Treasure Types

Monsters and lairs reference a lettered **Treasure Type** (A, B, C, … up to individual/lair variants)
that defines the chance and quantity of coins, gems, jewelry, and magic items found. When generating
loot for an encounter, look up the monster's treasure type rather than inventing amounts freehand.

## Gold Piece = XP

OSRIC uses the classic gold-piece-to-experience-point conversion: treasure recovered (and brought
back safely, per the table's ruling on "banked" treasure) converts to XP at a 1:1 gp-to-XP rate,
alongside XP from defeating monsters. Track both sources separately when awarding XP.

## Magic Item Identification

- Items are not automatically identified. Use in-fiction discovery (trial and error, a *identify*
  spell, sages, or a bard's lore check where applicable) rather than telling the player what an item
  does immediately upon pickup.
- Cursed items should be discoverable through play, not simply announced — let consequences reveal them.

## Encumbrance

Track carried weight against the character's Strength-based capacity. Overloaded characters suffer
reduced movement rate, which matters for wandering monster checks and fleeing. Don't hand-wave this
for parties hauling significant treasure or bulky loot out of a dungeon.

## Awarding Loot

1. Look up the treasure type for the encounter/lair.
2. Roll for composition (coins by type, gems, jewelry, magic items) per the treasure type table.
3. Note any identification requirements or curses for magic items (DM-only until discovered).
4. Record the haul in `campaigns/[name]/state.md` and update character sheets once divided.
