# NPC Generation & Roleplaying

Reference: `osric-srd/markdown/13 hirelings and henchmen.md` for hiring rules,
`osric-srd/markdown/17a monsters statistics men demi-humans.md` for human/demi-human NPC stat blocks.

## Reaction Rolls

When the party meets an NPC or monster group with no predetermined disposition, roll 2d6 on the
reaction table (modified by Charisma, prior reputation, offered gifts, etc.):

| 2d6 | Reaction |
|-----|----------|
| 2 | Hostile, attacks |
| 3–5 | Unfriendly, may act badly |
| 6–8 | Uncertain, neutral |
| 9–11 | Friendly, positive |
| 12 | Enthusiastic, helpful |

Roll this secretly and play the result out through the NPC's dialogue and behavior rather than
announcing the number.

## Stat Blocks

Use the standard OSRIC monster/NPC stat line: **AC, Movement (MV), Hit Dice (HD), hp, Number of
Attacks (#AT), Damage (DMG), Special Attacks (SA), Special Defenses (SD), Magic Resistance (MR),
Size (SZ), Alignment (AL), Morale (ML), XP value**. For named NPCs, add class/level, equipment, and
notable spells/abilities.

## Hirelings & Henchmen

- **Hirelings**: Paid specialists (torchbearers, guides, mercenaries, sages) — loyalty tracked
  loosely, driven by pay and treatment.
- **Henchmen**: Loyal followers, often lower-level adventurers, recruited via roleplay and a loyalty
  check. Track a loyalty score/modifier; poor treatment, unequal treasure shares, or abandonment in
  danger can trigger desertion or betrayal.
- Both count toward "Hirelings and Henchmen" rules in `osric-srd/markdown/13 hirelings and henchmen.md`
  for costs, availability, and limits (Charisma affects max henchmen).

## Roleplaying Guidance

- Give NPCs a clear want and a clear limit (what they won't do). Play both consistently.
- Use reaction rolls and morale as constraints on NPC behavior, not just flavor — an NPC that fails
  morale should visibly act on it (flee, surrender, break).
- Keep DM-only information (true motives, hidden stats, secret loyalties) out of narration; reveal
  only what the characters could plausibly perceive.
