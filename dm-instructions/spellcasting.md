# Spellcasting

Reference: `osric-srd/markdown/05 spells overview.md` for the memorization system, and the class-
specific spell lists (`06a`/`06b` Cleric, `07` Druid, `08`–`10` Magic-User, `11` Illusionist).

## Vancian Memorization

- Spellcasters (Cleric, Druid, Magic-User, Illusionist, and casting sub-classes like Paladin/Ranger
  at higher levels) must **memorize** spells in advance, typically after rest and study/prayer time.
- Casting a memorized spell **expends it** — it must be re-memorized (or re-prayed for) before it can
  be cast again.
- Magic-Users/Illusionists need their spellbook present to memorize (not to cast); Clerics/Druids
  pray for spells and don't require a book.
- Track each character's memorized spell list in their character sheet and cross it off as spells
  are cast during the session.

## Components

Spells may require Verbal (V), Somatic (S), and/or Material (M) components. A bound/gagged or
silenced caster cannot cast V or S spells; missing a required material component prevents casting
that spell entirely. Note material components with limited/costly supply (e.g. diamond dust) as
consumed on cast.

## Casting Time & Interruption

- Spells take a number of segments (or longer) to cast, tracked within the combat round.
- A caster who takes damage or is forced to move/lose concentration before the casting completes
  loses the spell (it is expended without effect) — apply this consistently in combat.

## Saving Throws vs. Spells

Most offensive spells allow a saving throw (category depends on the spell — often "Spell", sometimes
"Breath Weapon" for area effects, etc.). State the category and let the player roll; don't resolve it
silently.

## Reversible Spells

Some spells have a reversed form (e.g. *cure light wounds* / *cause light wounds*). Only clerics/druids
of appropriate alignment or training can typically cast the reversed form — confirm per spell entry
rather than assuming both directions are always available.

## Multi-Class Casters

Multi-classed spellcasters (e.g. Fighter/Magic-User) advance spell slots according to their caster
class level only, and may be restricted from casting in heavy armor (Magic-User/Illusionist spells)
— confirm the class combination's restrictions in `03b character classes magic user-multiclass.md`.
